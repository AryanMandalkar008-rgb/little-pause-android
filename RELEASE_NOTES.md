## Little Pause v1.4

- Added Today, History, and Goals tabs with a 7-day chart and 30-day review.
- Added daily motivational quotes and optional local suggestion reminders.
- Reduced accidental scroll counts by filtering small vertical movement and debouncing events.
- Added shortcuts for Accessibility, overlay, and battery settings.
- Updated the app icon with the provided brain and lightbulb design.

### Known limitations

Reel counting is an estimate based on large vertical accessibility scroll events. App versions that do not expose scroll distance may not count reliably. The limit screen blocks the entire supported app. Android and phone-maker battery settings can still stop background services.
